Small skills that prevent waste

⚡

Check the power path

Look for damaged cords, tripped fuses, and loose plugs before taking anything apart.

🧰

Use a safe toolkit

Basic screwdrivers, pliers, a multimeter, and a flashlight are enough for many fixes.

🧼

Clean before replacing

Dust, lint, and blocked vents are often the simple cause behind “dead” appliances.

📘

Know when to stop

Power, gas, or refrigerant issues should be handled by a qualified repairer.

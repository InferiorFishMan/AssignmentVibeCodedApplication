This lo-fi flow translates two Week 3 interviews into a practical first step, clear safety boundaries, and a choice of where to get help.

02 — PrivacyAnother preferred a private mechanic and repairs at their own organization.

Small sample: two interviews. These are design signals to test, not universal claims about appliance owners.
